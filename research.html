Research

Connecting spatial patterns with the economics of forest management.

Fuel treatment gaps on private forestlands

I examine the spatial distribution of fuel treatments and untreated high-risk private forestlands in Colorado, with attention to access to wood-processing facilities.

Biomass markets and treatment feasibility

My interests include how transportation costs, processing infrastructure, and biomass markets influence the economic feasibility of fuel treatments.

Federal fuel treatments and facility access

My master's research examined relationships between proximity to wood-processing facilities, hazardous fuel treatments, and biomass utilization on federal lands in Colorado.

Wildfire prevention education

I also work on research examining relationships between wildfire prevention education activities and human-caused wildfire occurrence.
