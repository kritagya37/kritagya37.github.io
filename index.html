About Me

Understanding the connections between forests, wildfire mitigation, and wood-product markets.

I am Kritagya Gyawali, a Ph.D. student in the Department of Forest and Rangeland Stewardship at Colorado State University, working with Dr. Srijana Baral.

My research examines how wood-processing infrastructure, biomass utilization, and economic conditions influence forest fuel treatment implementation. I combine spatial analysis and economic methods to study opportunities for forest management and wildfire mitigation.

Research interests

  • Forest economics and biomass utilization
  • Fuel treatment gaps and wildfire mitigation
  • Market access and wood-processing infrastructure
  • Spatial analysis, econometrics, and forest-management optimization
